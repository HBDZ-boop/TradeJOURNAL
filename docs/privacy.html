Rewind Journal Privacy policy Last updated 8 October 2026 Rewind Journal keeps your trading journal on your own computer. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. We cannot see your trades, because they are never sent anywhere. What stays on your PC Everything you put into Rewind is stored in a single folder on your own machine, by default Documents\Rewind: Your trades — prices, sizes, R multiples, profit and loss, dates, instruments, and every tag you apply Your notes on each trade and your weekly reviews Screenshots and chart images you attach Your pre-session check-ins — the sleep, focus, mood and stress ratings, and anything you write alongside them Your prop firm account rules and progress against them Your settings and CSV exports These are ordinary files that belong to you. You can open, copy, back up or delete them without going through Rewind, and uninstalling Rewind does not remove them. Voice dictation Rewind can turn speech into text in any notes field. When you hold the microphone button: Audio is captured into memory and transcribed on your own computer, by a speech model stored on your machine. No audio is ever written to disk, by Rewind or by anything it uses. No audio is ever transmitted. Dictation works with the internet switched off entirely. Only the resulting text is kept, in the notes field you put it in. The microphone is only ever opened while you are holding the button, and is closed again the moment you release it. When Rewind uses the internet Rewind works fully offline. There are exactly three reasons it ever connects, and none of them send your journal: What When What is sent Speech model download Once, and only if you choose to use voice dictation and accept the download prompt An ordinary file download from Hugging Face. Nothing of yours is uploaded. Update check On launch, in the version downloaded from GitHub only. The Microsoft Store version does not do this — the Store handles its own updates. A request to the public GitHub releases list. Nothing of yours is uploaded. Crash reports Only if you switch them on. They are off by default. See below. Opening the feedback form or a release page sends you to your own web browser. Rewind does not pass anything about you along with the link. Crash reports Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on, in the help menu. If you do turn it on, a report contains the error and where in the program it happened — and nothing else. Specifically, Rewind strips the following from every report before it leaves your computer: All local variables from the error, which is where a trade, a note or a filename could otherwise appear Any request contents Any user identifier Any extra context attached to the error No trade data, journal contents, check-in notes, file paths of your documents or anything identifying you is included in a crash report. Rewind has an automated test that fails the build if any of that could survive the stripping. What Rewind never does No user accounts, no sign-in, no password No cloud sync and no server holding your data No analytics, no telemetry, no usage tracking No advertising and no advertising identifiers No selling or sharing of data with anyone, because there is none to share No cookies — Rewind is a desktop application, not a website Children Rewind is a tool for adults keeping a record of their own trading. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone. Your rights Data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. In Rewind's case these are satisfied directly: your data is already in your possession, in files you control. To export it, use the CSV export or copy the folder. To delete it, delete the folder. There is nothing held about you anywhere else, so there is no request to make and nobody to make it to. Changes If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the public history of the repository linked below.
